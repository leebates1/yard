    TF JONES · DOOR KNOWLEDGE

    More than just the door leaf

    Performance depends on the complete assembly: the leaf, frame, seals, glazing and ironmongery must match the product’s supporting evidence and installation instructions.

    Which rating does a project need?

    Use the building’s fire strategy and the project specification. FD30 and FD60 are performance ratings, not interchangeable product choices.

    Look for traceability

    Check the product’s certification, identification and documentation. These connect the installed door to its tested specification.

    Learning overview · Source: BWF Fire Door Alliance, certification approach and best-practice guide.
